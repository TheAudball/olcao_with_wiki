## Welcome to the OLCAO wiki!

**OLCAO** stands for **O**rthagonalized **L**inear **C**ombination of **A**tomic **O**rbitals. It is a [Density Functional Theory (DFT)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Density_functional_theory) based *ab initio* method that uses periodic boundary conditions and atomic orbitals expanded as Gaussian functions as a basis to provide a fast and accurate description of the electronic structure, optical properties, etc. of a wide variety of atomic scale condensed matter and biological systems. OLCAO has been developed over the last ~40 years in the Physics and Astronomy [Graduate](https://sse.umkc.edu/academics/programs/physics-and-astronomy-graduate.html) and [Undergraduate](https://sse.umkc.edu/academics/programs/physics-and-astronomy.html) Programs at the [University of Missouri - Kansas City](http://umkc.edu/), under the guidance of professor emeritus Wai-Yim Ching while he led the Electronic Structure Group and professor [Paul Rulis](https://sse.umkc.edu/profiles/rulis-paul.html), the head of the [Computational Physics Group](https://info.umkc.edu/cpg/).
